**[2026-02-20] Thesis refined — "doubly worst-case" framing corrected.**
- Discovery: I(K;C) (diagnostic information about which component failed) and FIM(λ) (statistical information about parameters) are DISTINCT quantities. Uniform masking minimizes I(K;C) but is NOT worst-case for FIM(λ).
- Key finding: Deterministic (derangement) masking at w=m-1 recovers the EXACT same FIM as w=1 (no masking). Information loss comes from the masking mechanism, not the cardinality.
- Verified numerically for m=3,4,5,6 (`research/verify_masking_optimality.R`).
- Paper edits: Removed "doubly worst-case" from 4 locations. Reframed uniform masking as "maximal symmetry benchmark."
- Thesis updated: novelty now correctly says "tractable symmetry benchmark (not a worst-case bound)."
- Open question: Should the deterministic masking result become a formal proposition?
